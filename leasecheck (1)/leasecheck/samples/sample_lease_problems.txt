RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE - FICTIONAL - FOR TESTING ONLY)

This Lease is made between Cherry Row Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Sample and Alex Example ("Tenants") for the premises at 1500 Example Street, Unit 2, Philadelphia, PA 19122.

1. TERM. The lease term begins August 1, 2027 and ends July 31, 2028. This Lease shall automatically renew for an additional twelve (12) month term unless Tenant gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the term.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,200.00, due on the first day of each month. Landlord may increase the rent at any time during the lease term with thirty (30) days written notice.

3. LATE FEES. If rent is not received by the 1st, Tenant shall pay a late fee of $50 per day until rent is paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,600.00, equal to three (3) months' rent, before move-in. $400.00 of the security deposit is non-refundable and will be retained as a cleaning and administrative fee. Landlord will return the remaining deposit within sixty (60) days after Tenant vacates the premises.

5. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE. Tenant is responsible for all repairs to the premises, including heating, plumbing, and electrical systems, regardless of cause.

6. LANDLORD ACCESS. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the premises at any time without notice for any reason.

7. LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, damage, or loss to Tenant or Tenant's property, including damage caused by Landlord's negligence.

8. ROOMMATES. All Tenants are jointly and severally liable for all rent, fees, and damages under this Lease.

9. LEGAL FEES. Tenant shall pay all of Landlord's attorney fees and court costs in any dispute arising under this Lease.

10. WAIVERS. Tenant waives the right to a notice to quit and agrees that Landlord may confess judgment against Tenant for possession and any amounts owed.

11. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for gas and electric. Landlord pays water and trash.
