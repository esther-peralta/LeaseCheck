RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE - FICTIONAL - FOR TESTING ONLY)

This Lease is made between Broad Street Homes LLC ("Landlord") and Taylor Sample ("Tenant") for the premises at 2000 Sample Avenue, Unit 1, Philadelphia, PA 19121.

1. TERM. The lease term begins August 1, 2027 and ends July 31, 2028. Either party may end the lease at the end of the term with sixty (60) days written notice.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,000.00, due on the first day of each month. Rent is fixed for the full lease term.

3. LATE FEES. If rent is not received by the 5th of the month, Tenant shall pay a one-time late fee of $50.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,000.00, equal to one (1) month's rent. Landlord will return the deposit, with a written itemized list of any deductions, within thirty (30) days after Tenant vacates and provides a forwarding address.

5. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE. Landlord is responsible for repairs to heating, plumbing, electrical, and structural systems. Tenant is responsible for damage caused by Tenant or Tenant's guests. Tenant should report repair needs in writing.

6. LANDLORD ACCESS. Landlord may enter the premises with at least twenty-four (24) hours notice, except in an emergency.

7. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electric. Landlord pays gas, water, and trash.
